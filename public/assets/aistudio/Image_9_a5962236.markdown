## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a warm, vibrant culinary identity centered around conscious cooking and mindful consumption. It merges the utility of an intelligent kitchen assistant with the sensory appeal of a high-end food publication. 

The aesthetic sits at the intersection of **Contemporary Editorial and Tactile Organicism**. Rather than looking like a clinical inventory database, the UI feels earthy, appetizing, and reassuring. Visual warmth counters kitchen anxiety, while crisp micro-details and organic geometry reinforce precision in portioning and recipe generation. Generous whitespace, parchment-textured neutral backdrops, and herb-inspired accents create an inviting, domestic atmosphere optimized for high-stress cooking environments and quick handheld navigation.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from garden produce, terracotta cookware, and natural unbleached parchment:

- **Primary (`#2E5A44`, Deep Sage)**: Serves as the bedrock brand color, conveying freshness, sustainability, and calm intelligence. Used for primary CTAs, active tab icons, brand anchors, and structural navigation.
- **Secondary (`#E05A36`, Warm Persimmon/Terracotta)**: Represents heat, appetite, and cooking action. Reserved for high-intent interactions, recipe generation highlights, and active culinary states.
- **Tertiary (`#4E9F6E`, Fresh Herb)**: Provides vivid contrast for positive feedback, successful inventory matches, high-compatibility percentages, and freshness confirmations.
- **Neutral Palette (`#FBF9F5` Canvas, `#F5F1E9` Ground, `#EFE9E0` Card Surfaces)**: Warm culinary creams replacing sterile digital whites. Provides comfort in high-glare kitchen environments.
- **Text & On-Surface (`#1C2826` Heading Dark Slate, `#2D3A37` Body Slate)**: Forest-tinged charcoals delivering optical contrast without the harshness of pure black.
